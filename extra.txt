#why we do app.use() in server.js, whats that app actually do
= app is basically your Express application/server object.
Express gives you an object containing methods that let you configure your server.
For example:
app.use(cors());
app.use(express.json());

Think:

                    Express App
                        │
        ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
        ↓               ↓               ↓
    cors()         express.json()    your routes
    middleware       middleware

#why inngest =  Inngest handles Clerk events in the background and reliably syncs user data with our database.
But isn't that just a webhook?
Clerk → your server webhook can also accomplish this.
The difference is that Inngest gives you infrastructure around the event:
Retries if your function fails
Background/asynchronous execution
Event handling
Better reliability for longer-running work
Ability to chain multiple steps/functions
Monitoring of function executions
